# Known issues and risks

- SQLite is local-development only. A content-free trial audit-event table and fixed trial-role checks exist, but the actor header is not authentication and must not be treated as formal authorization, persistent production audit, retention/deletion policy, or production-security certification.
- Experience-case content can contain sensitive customer-service text. The new C modules avoid logging it, but `v1-reminder-only` is not automatic redaction and the internal-test reviewer identity is not authenticated. Do not submit real customer data until a separately frozen authentication, authorization, retention, access-audit, and redaction design is implemented.
- Approved snapshots are immutable in C, but the local SQLite implementation is a development/test foundation and is not a production audit, recovery, retention, or privacy-security certification.
- CORS is browser-origin control only, not authentication, authorization, CSRF protection, or a production-security guarantee.
- AI feedback v1 has only a pre-submission/confirmation redaction reminder. It has no automatic redaction, formal identity verification, authorization, retention policy, or production audit guarantee; the A export must remain limited to synthetic or already de-identified content.
- Feedback-case `revision=1` is intentionally immutable in this supplement. Re-analysis, revision correction, and hash/field evolution are blocked pending a separately frozen versioned contract.
- `created_by` and `Idempotency-Key` scope replay and history filtering but are not authentication or authorization. A production rollout requires separately frozen identity, authorization, audit, retention, and privacy controls.
- The prior A-line module detail and online-test contract/schema remains absent. The AI capability center controls remain unavailable/disabled rather than issuing guessed requests.
- Test catalog `publishable` source fields do not imply publication; package-level `production_usable=false` and `allow_auto_send=false` remain enforced by the baseline data model.
- The daily operations snapshot is a C-internal trial/test aggregation with UTC-day semantics. It is not a timezone-local business report, formal service-level monitor, authentication boundary, or production operational report. Missing sources must remain visible as unavailable rather than being inferred from another metric.
